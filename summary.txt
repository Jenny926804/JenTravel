Destination & Visa Information
------------------------------

Egypt – Sharm El Sheikh
• Visa: Available on arrival for Lebanese passport holders (up to 30 days).  
• Flights: Direct charter flights from Beirut.  
• Best Time to Visit: March to June, September to November.  
• Highlights: Red Sea resorts, diving, desert safaris, and relaxation.

Europe – Schengen Zone (France, Italy, Netherlands, etc.)
• Visa: Schengen visa required; handled through our partnership with TLS Visa Application Center in Beirut.  
• Flights: Daily connections via MEA, Turkish Airlines, Air France, and others.  
• Best Time to Visit: April to October.  
• Highlights: Historic cities, museums, culture, and cuisine.

Indonesia – Bali
• Visa: Visa on arrival for Lebanese travelers (valid 30 days).  
• Flights: Beirut → Dubai / Doha → Denpasar (1 stop).  
• Best Time to Visit: April to October (dry season).  
• Highlights: Beaches, temples, yoga, and local cuisine.

Turkey – Cappadocia
• Visa: E-visa or visa on arrival available for Lebanese citizens.  
• Flights: Beirut → Istanbul → Kayseri.  
• Best Time to Visit: April to June, September to October.  
• Highlights: Hot air balloons, cave hotels, and cultural tours.

Morocco – Marrakech & Fes
• Visa: Visa-free entry for Lebanese travelers (up to 90 days).  
• Flights: Beirut → Istanbul / Doha → Casablanca.  
• Best Time to Visit: March to May, September to November.  
• Highlights: Medina markets, Sahara desert tours, and riad stays.

Lebanon – Local Escapes
• Visa: N/A for residents and citizens.  
• Best Time to Visit: Year-round, especially summer weekends.  
• Highlights: Byblos heritage walks, Cedars hiking, Tyre beaches, and wine trails.
